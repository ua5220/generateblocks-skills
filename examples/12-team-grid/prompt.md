# Team Grid Prompt

## The Prompt

> Create a team section with "Meet Our Team" heading and 4 team member cards. Each card should have a photo, name, role, short bio, and social links (LinkedIn, Twitter). Cards should have a sophisticated hover effect that reveals the bio with a slide-up animation. Use a dark overlay on hover. Mobile responsive with 2x2 grid on tablet and single column on mobile.

## Design Notes

- Section padding: 5rem top/bottom
- Background: White
- Card: Rounded corners, overflow hidden
- Photo: Square aspect ratio, object-fit cover
- Hover: Dark overlay slides up revealing bio and social links
- Social icons: Small circular buttons
- Mobile: Stack to single column
