# Timeline Prompt

## The Prompt

> Create a vertical timeline section showing 4 process steps or milestones. Alternating left/right layout on desktop with a center line. Each step has a numbered circle, title, description, and optional date/label. Include connecting line with dots. Collapse to single column on mobile with line on the left side.

## Design Notes

- Section padding: 5rem top/bottom
- Background: White
- Center line: Thin vertical line with gradient
- Step circles: Numbered, accent color on active/current
- Alternating: Even items on right, odd items on left
- Mobile: All items on right side of line
- Animations: Fade in on scroll (CSS-only)
