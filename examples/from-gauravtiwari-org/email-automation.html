Philosophy

How I Think About Email

01

One Job Per Email

Each email has one purpose. Educate, build trust, or make an offer. Never all three. Clarity converts. Confusion kills sales.

02

Human, Not Brand

Nobody opens emails from companies. They open emails from people. Your sequences should sound like a smart friend, not a press release.

03

Behavior-Based Triggers

The best automations respond to what people do, not arbitrary timers. Clicked a pricing link? Get a different email than someone who hasn't.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you write the emails?

Yes. Strategy, copy, subject lines, CTAs. You review and approve. If you have existing copy you love, I can work with that. But most clients find my copy outperforms what they had.

What if I already have a platform?

I work with whatever you have. If your current platform is limiting you, I'll tell you honestly. But I'm not going to recommend switching just because I prefer a different tool.

How long until I see results?

Depends on your list size and traffic. If you have leads flowing in, you can see results within the first week. I set up proper tracking so you know exactly what's working.

What about deliverability?

It's part of every project. Domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). List hygiene. Proper sending practices. Beautiful automation is worthless if nobody sees it.

Can you integrate with my tools?

Almost always yes. CRM, website, payment processor, webinar platform, course platform. If something doesn't have native integration, I build connections through Make or Zapier.

Will emails feel impersonal?

Not if done right. I write in your voice, use merge tags appropriately, and design sequences that respond to subscriber behavior. Your subscribers shouldn't be able to tell the difference.
