The Real Problem

Pretty Websites That Don't Make Money Are Just Expensive Art

I have seen it too many times. A business spends ₹2-3 lakhs on a beautiful website redesign. Gorgeous typography. Stunning photography. Smooth animations. Awards-worthy aesthetics.

Six months later? Same conversion rate as before. Sometimes worse.

The designer focused on making something that looked good in their portfolio. Not something that actually worked for the business.

Good UI design is not about making things pretty. It is about making things clear. When someone lands on your page, do they immediately understand what you do, why they should care, and what to do next?

That is the job of UI design. Everything else is decoration.

I redesigned a landing page for a coaching institute. The original had a hero image of their building, a paragraph about their history, and a "Learn More" button. The new design? Clear value proposition in 8 words, social proof with 4,200+ students, specific outcomes achieved, and a "Book Free Session" button. Same content, restructured. Conversion rate: 1.2% → 4.8%.

I have been designing interfaces since 2008. I have watched trends come and go. You know what never goes out of style? Interfaces that make it easy for people to do what they came to do.

Philosophy

How I Approach Design

Not abstract principles. Specific decisions I make on every project.

Clarity Over Cleverness

Your headline should say what you do. Your buttons should say what they do. Your layout should make the hierarchy obvious. If visitors have to think about what something means, you have already lost.

Mobile-First, Not Mobile-Also

70%+ of your visitors are on phones. I do not design for desktop and then squeeze it into mobile. I start with mobile, where constraints force better decisions, then expand for larger screens.

Conversion Over Aesthetics

I will argue against a beautiful design element if it hurts conversions. That stunning video background? It is slowing your page. That creative navigation? It is confusing people. I prioritize what works.

Speed Is A Design Decision

Every image, font, animation adds weight. I design with performance in mind. Light font stacks. Optimized images. Purposeful animations. Fast pages convert better. Period.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you also build the website?

Yes. I am a developer first, designer second. I can design and build end-to-end. If you have your own developer, I deliver files they can work with. Either way works.

What if I do not like the design?

That is what wireframes and revisions are for. We align on structure before I invest time in visuals. If the final design misses the mark, we revise until it works.

Can you match an existing brand?

Absolutely. If you have brand guidelines, I follow them. If you have a logo and colors but no formal guidelines, I build a cohesive visual system around what exists.

How many revisions are included?

Each package specifies revision rounds. Revisions mean changes to existing designs, not complete direction changes. I am clear about this upfront so there are no surprises.

What do you need from me?

A clear understanding of your business, customers, and goals. Examples of sites you like (and do not like). Brand assets if you have them. Content for the pages, at least in draft form.

How long does it take?

Landing page: 5-7 days. Marketing website: 2-3 weeks. Complex projects depend on scope. I provide realistic timelines upfront and stick to them.
