The Real Problem

Most Online Stores Are Designed to Fail

I have seen it hundreds of times. Someone launches an ecommerce store. They pick a nice-looking theme. Add their products. Connect a payment gateway. Wait for sales.

Six months later, they are wondering why their conversion rate is stuck at 0.8% when the industry average is 2-3%.

The answer is almost always the same: the store was built like a brochure, not a sales machine.

Think about your last online purchase. You probably did not even notice the dozens of small design decisions that guided you from "just looking" to "order confirmed." The urgency cues. The trust signals. The frictionless checkout. The mobile optimization that did not make you pinch and zoom.

Those things do not happen by accident. They are engineered.

The Math Is Simple

If you are getting 1,000 visitors per month with a 1% conversion rate, you make 10 sales. Improve that to 2.5% with proper store optimization, and you are at 25 sales. Same traffic. 150% more revenue. That is what good ecommerce development actually delivers.

I have built stores for luxury fashion brands, education platforms selling courses, B2B suppliers with complex pricing, and D2C brands competing against giants like Amazon. The fundamentals are the same: make it easy to buy, make it feel trustworthy, make it fast.

Platforms

eCommerce Stores I Build

Platform-agnostic. I recommend what fits your business, not what pays me the highest commission.

WooCommerce

My specialty. Full control, no platform fees eating your margins, and WordPress flexibility for content marketing. Best for businesses that want to own their store completely.

Ideal for: Indian businesses (Razorpay, PayU integration), content-driven brands, B2B with custom pricing, complex product configurations.

Shopify

When you want to focus purely on selling without worrying about hosting, security, or updates. Higher monthly costs but lower maintenance burden.

Ideal for: D2C brands scaling fast, dropshipping, international sales with multi-currency, businesses wanting done-for-you infrastructure.

FluentCart

The new kid that is surprisingly powerful. Native WordPress integration, blazing fast, excellent for digital products and subscriptions. Lower complexity than WooCommerce.

Ideal for: Course creators, membership sites, digital downloads, SaaS billing, simple physical product stores.

The Difference

What Makes My Stores Convert

Not marketing buzzwords. Specific things I obsess over because the data shows they move the needle.

1

Speed That Sells

Every second of load time costs you 7% of conversions. I build stores that load in under 2 seconds on mobile. Server-side rendering, optimized images, efficient code, proper caching. Your customers never wait.

2

Frictionless Checkout

Average cart abandonment is 70%. Most happens at checkout. I build single-page checkout, auto-filled addresses, multiple payment methods including UPI, cards, wallets, and EMI. Trust badges where hesitation happens.

3

Product Pages That Sell

Your product page is your sales pitch. I structure with clear hierarchy: the hook (headline, hero image), the proof (reviews, trust signals), the details (specs, benefits), and the close (clear CTA).

4

Mobile-First Experience

Over 70% of Indian ecommerce happens on mobile. I design mobile-first: touch-friendly buttons, thumb-zone navigation, simplified checkout, images optimized for varying network conditions.

5

Trust Signals Everywhere

Online shoppers are skeptical. I integrate trust signals throughout: secure payment badges, real customer reviews, clear return policies, live chat or WhatsApp support. Each signal reduces perceived risk.

6

Revenue Recovery

Abandoned cart emails, back-in-stock notifications, browse abandonment sequences. Automated systems that recover lost sales while you sleep. Most stores leave 30% of revenue on the table.

FAQ

Common Questions

WooCommerce or Shopify?

India + full control + lower ongoing costs = WooCommerce. Managed infrastructure + okay with fees = Shopify. Digital products = consider FluentCart. I give honest recommendations based on your situation.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes. Shopify to WooCommerce, Magento to WooCommerce, anything. Products, customers, orders, reviews all migrate. Redirects set up so you don't lose SEO value.

What about ongoing maintenance?

Maintenance retainers start at ₹20,000/month covering updates, security monitoring, minor changes, and priority support. Most stores need at least quarterly maintenance.

Do you handle hosting?

I recommend and configure hosting as part of the project. Cloudways, Runcloud, or custom Hetzner/DigitalOcean setups based on your traffic and budget. No commissions, just what works best.

What if I need changes after launch?

Changes within scope during support period are included. Beyond that, hourly work (₹3,000/hour) or monthly retainers. Most clients prefer a small retainer for ongoing improvements.

How long does it take?

Store Launch: 2 weeks. Growth Store: 4 weeks. Custom builds depend on scope. I provide realistic timelines upfront and stick to them.
